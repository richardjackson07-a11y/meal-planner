# Marry Me Chicken | 4 | 35 min | chicken, comfort
2 lb chicken breast
1 cup heavy cream
1/2 cup sun dried tomatoes
1/2 cup parmesan
3 cloves garlic
2 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp italian seasoning
1 tsp paprika
1 bunch fresh basil
1 lb baby potatoes
> Season and sear the chicken hard, 5 min a side, set aside.
> Same pan: garlic, sun dried tomatoes, cream, parmesan, simmer to coat a spoon.
> Chicken back in with the basil. Roast the potatoes alongside at 425F.

# Green Chile Chicken Enchiladas | 5 | 50 min | chicken, tex-mex, comfort
2 lb chicken thighs
12 corn tortillas
28 oz green enchilada sauce
3 cups shredded cheddar
1 cup sour cream
1 onion
4 cloves garlic
1 can green chiles
1 bunch cilantro
1 tsp cumin
> Poach and shred the chicken, mix with sour cream, chiles, half the cheese.
> Roll in warmed tortillas, cover with sauce and the rest of the cheese.
> 375F for 25 min until the edges bubble.

# Chicken Fried Steak | 4 | 45 min | beef, comfort, southern
2 lb cube steak
2 cups flour
2 eggs
1 cup buttermilk
2 cups vegetable oil
2 cups milk
1 tsp paprika
1 tsp garlic powder
salt and black pepper
2 lb baby potatoes
> Dredge flour, egg wash, flour again. Rest 10 min so it sticks.
> Fry in 350F oil, 4 min a side. Drain.
> Gravy from 3 tbsp of the drippings, 3 tbsp flour, milk, lots of black pepper.

# Slow Cooker Barbacoa | 6 | 8 hr | beef, slow cooker, tex-mex
4 lb chuck roast
4 chipotle peppers in adobo
1 cup beef broth
6 cloves garlic
2 limes
1 onion
1 tbsp cumin
2 tsp oregano
3 bay leaves
16 corn tortillas
> Sear the roast, then everything into the slow cooker on low for 8 hours.
> Shred, pull the bay leaves, and let it sit in its own juice 10 min.
> Serve in charred tortillas with raw onion and lime.

# Honey Garlic Pork Tenderloin | 4 | 35 min | pork, fast

1/3 cup honey
1/4 cup soy sauce
6 cloves garlic
2 tbsp butter
1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
1 lb green beans
1 lb baby potatoes
> Sear the tenderloin on all sides, then 400F for 15 min to 145F inside.

> Rest 10 min before slicing or it all runs out.

# Cacio e Pepe | 4 | 20 min | pasta, fast
1 lb spaghetti
2 cups parmesan
3 tbsp black pepper
4 tbsp butter
> Toast the cracked pepper in butter while the pasta boils.
> Save two cups of pasta water. This is the whole trick.
> Off the heat, cheese and water alternately, tossing hard until it turns glossy.
